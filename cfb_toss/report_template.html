Taking the ball first

How often do power-conference teams that win the opening coin toss choose to receive instead of deferring, who does it, and does it change results?

01

The short version

    02

    It has been flat for six seasons

    Roughly one toss winner in six takes the ball. The rate has held between 16% and 20% every season, including after the 2023 rule change that kept the clock running after first downs.

    03

    It is a coach decision, and a rare one

    Most programs almost never receive. A handful do it nearly every time. A team's rate at home and on the road correlates at 0.81, so this follows the head coach, not the stadium or stat crew.

    04

    When the coach changes, the tendency changes

    Season-by-season receives over tosses won. Sample sizes per season are small, but the switches line up with coaching changes.

    Coaching notes are added for context and are not derived from the data set. Iowa's swings under a single head coach (Kirk Ferentz) suggest some coaches decide game by game.

    05

    Situations barely move it

    Home or away, favorite or underdog, the choice rate stays within a few points of 18%. Two modest exceptions are heavy favorites (21+ points) and games against non-power opponents, where teams take the ball a bit more often. The SEC is the only conference noticeably above the rest, driven by Ole Miss and Mississippi State.

    06

    Does taking the ball help?

    Not on the scoreboard at the end. Teams that receive are slightly better on average (favored by 7.1 points versus 5.2 for deferrers), so results are measured against the point spread. On that basis neither choice wins more than expected, and both cover about half the time.

    The choice does shift when the points come. Receivers run about 2 points ahead of expectation at halftime and give about the same back after the break, when the deferring opponent gets the first possession.

    07

    Data and method

    Source: ESPN's public play-by-play, which logs the referee's coin-toss call as a play (for example, Michigan won the toss and has deferred to the second half). Games involving at least one team from the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC, Pac-12 (through 2023) or Notre Dame were included, with conference membership assigned season by season. Spreads come from ESPN's betting feed. Each stated decision was checked against which team actually took the opening kickoff.
