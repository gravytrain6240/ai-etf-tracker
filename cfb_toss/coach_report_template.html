Taking the ball first

    03

    The coaches who take the ball

    Head coaches with at least 12 verified tosses won and a receive rate of 40% or higher. The typical FBS coach takes the ball about one time in six.

    04

    Coaches who changed schools

    Receives over verified tosses won at each stop. Most coaches bring their habit with them. A few, notably Lane Kiffin and Neal Brown, changed it at the new job, at least so far.

    05

    Schools that changed coaches

    The same program flips when the head coach changes. Interim coaches are included.

    10

    How complete the data is

    Toss calls come from ESPN's play-by-play, which logs the referee's call, for example Michigan won the toss and has deferred to the second half. About one game in seven was logged by ESPN in a stat-crew format that names the receiving team as the toss winner. Those games, mostly SEC and ACC home games and bowls from 2022–2024, were filled from the NCAA's official stat-crew play-by-play on NCAA.com. Every call is checked against which team actually took the opening kickoff. Head coaches come from CollegeFootballData.com.
