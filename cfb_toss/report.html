Taking the ball first

When a team wins the opening coin toss, who decides to take the ball instead of deferring: the head coach or the program?

The short version

    02

    The coach decides, not the school

    Correlation between each decision (receive = 1, defer = 0) and the receive rate from the other source. Log-loss: coach 0.392 vs. school 0.525 (lower is better).

    03

    The coaches who take the ball

    Head coaches with at least 12 verified tosses won and a receive rate of 40% or higher. The typical FBS coach takes the ball about one time in six.

    04

    Coaches who changed schools

    Receives over verified tosses won at each stop. Most coaches bring their habit with them. A few, notably Lane Kiffin and Neal Brown, changed it at the new job, at least so far.

    05

    Schools that changed coaches

    The same program flips when the head coach changes. Interim coaches are included.

    06

    How complete the data is

    Every toss comes from ESPN's play-by-play, which logs the referee's call, for example Michigan won the toss and has deferred to the second half. Each call is checked against which team actually took the opening kickoff. Head coaches come from CollegeFootballData.com.
